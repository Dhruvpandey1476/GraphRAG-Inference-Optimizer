A NEW PROOF OF FALTINGS’ LOCAL-GLOBAL PRINCIPLE FOR THE FINITENESS OF LOCAL COHOMOLOGY MODULES  arXiv:1406.1323v2 [math.AC] 16 Jun 2014  DAVOOD ASADOLLAHI AND REZA NAGHIPOUR∗ Abstract. Let R denote a commutative Noetherian ring. Brodmann et al. in [5] defined and studied the concept of the local-global principle for annihilation of local cohomology modules at level r ∈ N for the ideals a and b of R. It was shown in [5] that this principle holds at levels 1,2, over R and at all levels whenever dim R ≤ 4. The goal f b (M) of this paper is to show that, if the set AssR (Ha a (M )) is finite or fa (M ) 6= cba (M ), then the local-global principle holds at all levels r ∈ N0 , for all ideals a, b of R and each finitely generated R-module M , where cba (M ) denotes the first non b-cofiniteness of local cohomology module Hai (M ). As a consequence of this, we provide a new and short proof of the Faltings’ local-global principle for finiteness dimensions. Also, several new results concerning the finiteness dimensions are given.  1. Introduction Throughout this paper, let R denote a commutative Noetherian ring (with identity) and a an ideal of R.
