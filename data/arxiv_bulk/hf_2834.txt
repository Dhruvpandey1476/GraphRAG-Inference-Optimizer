In this article we mainly consider the positively Z– graded polynomial ring R = F[X, Y ] over an arbitrary field F and Hilbert series of finitely generated graded R–modules. The central result is an arithmetic criterion for such a series to be the Hilbert series of some R–module of positive depth. In the generic case, that is deg(X) and deg(Y ) being coprime, this criterion can be formulated in terms of the numerical semigroup generated by those degrees.  1.
