Monakhov January 23, 2018 Abstract We prove that if a group G = AB is the mutually permutable product of the supersoluble subgroups A and B, then the supersoluble residual of G coincides with the nilpotent residual of the derived subgroup G′ . Keywords: finite groups, supersoluble subgroup, mutually permutable product. MSC2010: 20D20, 20E34  All groups in this paper are finite. If F is a formation and G is a group, then GF is the F-residual of G, i.e., the smallest normal subgroup of G with quotient in F.
