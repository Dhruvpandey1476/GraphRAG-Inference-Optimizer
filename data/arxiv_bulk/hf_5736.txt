arXiv:1301.2419v1 [math.AC] 11 Jan 2013  ŁOJASIEWICZ INEQUALITY OVER THE RING OF POWER SERIES IN TWO VARIABLES GUILLAUME ROND Abstract. We prove a Łojasiewicz type inequality for a system of polynomial equations with coefficients in the ring of formal power series in two variables. This result is an effective version of the Strong Artin Approximation Theorem. From this result we deduce a bound of Artin functions of isolated singularities.  1.
