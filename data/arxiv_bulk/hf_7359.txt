arXiv:1803.11204v1 [math.RT] 29 Mar 2018  INFINITE DIMENSIONAL CHEVALLEY GROUPS AND KAC–MOODY GROUPS OVER Z LISA CARBONE AND DONGWEN LIU  Abstract. Let A be a symmetrizable generalized Cartan matrix. Let g be the corresponding Kac– Moody algebra over a commutative ring R with 1. We construct an infinite dimensional analog GV of a finite dimensional Chevalley group over R, constructed using integrable highest weight modules V , a Z–form of the universal enveloping algebra of g and a Z–form VZ . Our construction naturally gives rise to ‘arithmetic’ subgroups GV (Z), which we also construct using representation theory. We prove a Bruhat decomposition GV (Q) = GV (Z)B(Q) over Q. We also consider a universal representationtheoretic Kac–Moody group G and prove that its arithmetic subgroup Γ(Z) coincides with the subgroup of integral points G(Z).  1. Introduction A Kac–Moody group is an abstract group G = G(A) associated to an infinite dimensional Kac–Moody algebra g with symmetrizable generalized Cartan matrix A. We view the group G as an infinite dimensional Lie group, though there is no well–defined notion of dimension in the currently known Kac–Moody group constructions. Over general commutative rings, the final definition of a Kac–Moody group has not yet been found, though affine Kac–Moody groups have been constructed over commutative rings [Ga1]. The methods for constructing Kac–Moody groups over fields, however, are numerous. Several authors have proposed group constructions using a variety of techniques and additional external data (see for example [CG, Ga1, GW, KP, Ku, Ma, M, MT, Sl, Ti1]). Many constructions use some version of the Tits functor [Ti2]. e from commutative rings to groups and showed that any other group functor G Tits defined a functor G e by way of axioms KMG1–KMG5 imitating the properties of Chevalley– which approximates his functor G e → G, which is an isomorphism at every Demazure group schemes, admits a functorial homomorphism G field [Ti2].  Here we describe a construction of a ‘representation theoretic’ Kac–Moody group GV , over any commutative ring with 1, constructed using an integrable highest weight module V for the underlying Kac–Moody algebra g, a Z–form of the universal enveloping algebra of g and a Z–form VZ . Tits refers to the possibility of making such a construction (item (b) on page 554 of [Ti2]). Our construction is a natural generalization of the theory of elementary Chevalley groups over commutative rings (as in [Ch, St], see for example [VP]) and builds on the seminal work of Garland ([Ga1]). A similar construction for GV was used in [CG] to construct Kac–Moody groups over arbitrary fields.  We give a detailed description of the integral form GV (Z) over Z. We prove a Bruhat decomposition GV (Q) = GV (Z)B(Q) over Q. We also consider a universal representation-theoretic Kac–Moody group G(Q), by taking into account all the irreducible integrable highest weight modules V λ simultaneously. Applying the Bruhat decomposition, we are able to prove that the arithmetic subgroup Γ(Z) of G(Q) Date: April 2, 2018. 2000 Mathematics subject classification. Our group constructions depend on choice of lattice Λ between the root lattice and weight lattice, an integrable highest weight module V and a dominant integral weight λ. In [CW], the authors obtained some preliminary results about the dependence of GV (Z) on λ for V = V λ . The details are discussed in Subsection 3.5. In the finite dimensional case, a choice of Chevalley basis for the underlying Lie algebra is a crucial tool for constructing Chevalley groups. This in turn requires determining structure constants for the Lie algebra. These notions make sense only on the root spaces of a Kac–Moody algebra for real roots whose sums are real. Construction of Chevalley bases and determining structure constants across a whole Kac–Moody algebra are challenging tasks which are being undertaken in several works in progress. Our motivation comes in part from several conjectures regarding discrete duality groups in high energy theoretical physics, in particular in supergravity and M–theory, where integral forms of Kac–Moody symmetry groups are conjectured to arise as a result of quantum effects ([BC, DHN, Ju1, Ju2, We]). The authors take great pleasure in thanking both. 2. Preliminaries Let A = (aij )i,j∈I be a generalized Cartan matrix. The entries of A satisfy the conditions aij ∈ Z, i, j ∈ I, aii = 2, i ∈ I, aij ≤ 0 if i 6= j, and aij = 0 ⇐⇒ aji = 0. We will assume throughout that A is symmetrizable: there exist positive rational numbers q1 , . . . , qℓ , such that the matrix DA is symmetric, where D = diag(q1 , . . . , qℓ ). We say that A is of finite type if A is positive definite and that A is of affine type if A is positivesemidefinite, but not positive-definite. If A is not of finite or affine type, we say that A has indefinite type. Among the indefinite types, we are interested in the case that A is of hyperbolic type: that is, A is neither of finite nor affine type, but every proper, indecomposable submatrix is either of finite or of affine type. In the case V λ is faithful, our group construction depends on (a) a choice of lattice Q ⊆ Λ ⊆ P between the root lattice Q and weight lattice P , and (b) a Z-form VZλ of the integrable highest weight module V λ . The lattice Λ can be realized as LV λ , the lattice of weights of the representation V λ , which is between Q and P ([St, Lemma 27]). In what follows however, we will primarily consider only the simply connected Kac–Moody group G = GV (R), which assumes a choice of integrable highest weight module V λ satisfying LV λ = P . This depends on the parameter λ, a dominant integral weight. When R is a field, Garland constructed affine Kac–Moody groups as central extensions of loop groups and characterized the dependence of a completion of GV λ (R) on λ in terms of the Steinberg cocycle [Ga1]. Recent work of Rousseau [Rou] shows that over fields of characteristic zero, completions of Kac–Moody groups (as in [RR, CG], see also [Mar]) are isomorphic as topological groups. The complete Kac–Moody groups of [CG] are constructed with respect to a choice of dominant integral weight λ. Thus over fields of characteristic zero, the complete Kac–Moody groups of [CG] are independent of λ up to isomorphism, as topological groups.
