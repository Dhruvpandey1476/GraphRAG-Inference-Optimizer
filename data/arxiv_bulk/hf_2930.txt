Independenly, studies of endomorphisms of totally disconnected groups have also been performed in [15] and [5]. Whereas the former deals with profinite groups, the latter has a view towards topological entropy. According to [5, Corollary 4.11] (combined with Proposition 9.5), the topological entropy htop (α) of an endomorphism α of a totally disconnected, locally compact group G is given by htop (α) = ln s(α), if con(α) is closed. Our Theorem C (c) therefore implies the following Addition Theorem for topological entropy, which is complementary to a similar, known result ([5, Theorem 1.2]): If con(α) is closed in G and H is a closed normal subgroup of G such that H ⊆ par− (G) and α(H) = H, then htop (α) = htop (α|H ) + htop (α). A map q : X → Y between topological spaces is called a quotient map if it is surjective and Y carries the quotient topology (the final topology with respect to q).
