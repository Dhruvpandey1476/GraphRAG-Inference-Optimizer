arXiv:1707.05906v3 [math.RA] 22 Jan 2018  HALF-AXES IN POWER ASSOCIATIVE ALGEBRAS YOAV SEGEV Abstract. Let A be a commutative, non-associative algebra over a field F of characteristic 6= 2. A half-axis in A is an idempotent e ∈ A such that e satisfies the Peirce multiplication rules in a Jordan algebra, and, in addition, the 1-eigenspace of ade (multiplication by e) is one dimensional. In this paper we consider the identities (∗) x2 x2 = x4 and x3 x2 = xx4 . We show that if identities (∗) hold strictly in A, then one gets (very) interesting identities between elements in the eigenspaces of ade (note that if |F| > 3 and the identities (∗) hold in A, then they hold strictly in A). Furthermore we prove that if A is a primitive axial algebra of Jordan type half (i.e., A is generated by half-axes), and the identities (∗) hold strictly in A, then A is a Jordan algebra.  1. Introduction Throughout this paper F is a field of characteristic not 2 and A is a commutative non-associative algebra over F. Given an element x ∈ A and a scalar λ ∈ F, we denoted: Aλ (x) := {y ∈ A | yx = λy} . (We allow Aλ (x) = 0.) Definition 1.1. Let e ∈ A, and set Z := A0 (e) and U := A1/2 (e). We say that e is a half-axis if and only if (1) e2 = e (so e is an idempotent). (2) A1 (e) = Fe. (3) A = Fe ⊕ U ⊕ Z. (4) Z 2 ⊆ Z, U 2 ⊆ Fe + Z and U Z ⊆ U. Note that any idempotent e in a Jordan algebra J such that J1 (e) = Fe is a half-axis. Recall that A is a primitive axial algebra of Jordan type half if A is generated (as an algebra over F) by half-axes. We also need the following notation. Scalar extension and strict validity of identities. For a field extension K/F, we denote by AK := A ⊗F K the scalar extension (or base change) of A from F to K, which is a commutative non-associative K-algebra in a natural way. It is well known that Jordan algebras are invariant under base change (see e.g. [M, Linearization Proposition 1.8.5(2), p. 148]), so A is a Jordan algebra over F if and only if AK is one over K. Moreover, since tensor products commute with direct sums, if e ∈ A is a half-axis, then e is a half-axis in AK . Since primitive axial algebras of Jordan type half are spanned by half-axes (see [HRS, Corollary 1.2, p. 81]), it follows that primitive axial algebras are stable under base change as well. But powerassociative algebras are not. For this reason, the concept of strict powerassociativity comes in: A is called strictly power-associative if the scalar extensions AK are power-associative, for all field extensions K/F. Similarly, an identity is said to hold strictly in A if it is satisfied not only by A but by all its scalar extensions. Our main result is the following theorem. Main Theorem. Let A be primitive axial algebra of Jordan type half generated over F (as an algebra) by a set A of half-axes. The following are equivalent. (i) A is a Jordan algebra. (ii) A is strictly power associative. (iii) The identities x2 x2 = xx3  and  x3 x2 = xx4  hold strictly in A. (iv) For all e ∈ A, u ∈ A1/2 (e) and z ∈ A0 (e) we have (a) u3 = δu2 u. (b) (uz)z = 21 uz 2 . The interesting implications of the Main Theorem are of course (iii) ⇒ (iv) and (iv) ⇒ (i). The implication (iii) ⇒ (iv) is part of Theorem 1.4 below, and the implication (iv) ⇒ (i) is proven in §3. Remark 1.3. Throughout this paper we will deal with the identities (1.1)  x2 x2 = xx3  and  x3 x2 = xx4 .  HALF-AXES IN POWER ASSOCIATIVE ALGEBRAS  3  Note that if |F| > 3 and the identities of equation (1.1) hold in A, then, by Corollary 2.3(2) below, these identities hold strictly in A.
