DUAL OF FALTINGS’ THEOREMS ON FINITENESS OF LOCAL COHOMOLOGY MARZIYEH HATAMKHANI  arXiv:1711.01579v1 [math.AC] 5 Nov 2017  Abstract. Let R be a commutative Noetherian ring and a an ideal of R. We intend to establish the dual of two Faltings’ Theorems for local homology modules of an Artinian module. As a consequence of this, we show that, if A is an Artinian module over semi-local complete ring R and j is an integer such that Hia (A) is Artinian for all i < j, then the set CoassR (Hja (A)) is finite. We also introduce the notion of the nth a (A) = inf{g aRp (p A) : p ∈ Spec(R) and dim R/p ≥ n}, for all n ∈ N and prove Artinianness dimension gn 0  that g1a (A) = inf{i ∈ N0 : Hia (A) is not minimax}, whenever R is a semi-local complete ring. Moreover, in this situation we show that CoassR (Hgaa (A) (A)) is a finite set. 1  1. Introduction Throughout this paper, R denotes a commutative Noetherian ring with nonzero identity, a an ideal of R and M an R-module. The theory of local cohomology was introduced by A. Grothendieck and developed so much after six decays by different commutative algebraists. While the local cohomology functors are studied in great detail not so much is known about the local homology functors. The local homology functors were first studied by Matlis [Mat] in 1974. Then the study of this theory was continued by work of Greenlees and May [GM] and Alonso Tarrı́o, Jeremı́as López and Lipman [AJL]. In [HD], the vanishing of local homology modules and also, in [CN] and in [Ta], the local homology of Artinian modules are studied. It is shown that local homology of Artinian modules admit a theory parallel to the theory for local cohomology of finitely generated modules. In section 2, we give some preliminaries on co-localization of an R- module and also local homology modules which are necessary to the next sections. Let M be a finitely generated R-module and let a be an ideal of R. An important theorem in local cohomology is Faltings’ Local- Global principle for the finiteness dimension of local cohomology modules i [F1], which states that for a positive integer r, the Rp -module HaR (Mp ) is finitely generated for all i ≤ r p  and for all p ∈ Spec(R) if and only if the R-module Hai (M ) is finitely generated for all i ≤ r. Recall that the finiteness dimension fa (M ) of M relative to a is defined by fa (M ) := inf{i ∈ N0 : Hai (M ) is not finitely generated} = inf{i ∈ N0 : a * Rad(0 :R Hai (M ))}, with the usual convention that the infimum of the empty set of integers is interpreted as ∞. Let b a second ideal of R such that b ⊆ a. The b-finiteness dimension fab (M ) of M relative to a is defined by fab (M ) := inf{i ∈ N0 : b * Rad(0 :R Hai (M ))}. 2010 Mathematics Subject Classification. 13D45; 13D07. Key words and phrases. Artinianness dimension; finiteness dimension; Local cohomology modules; Local homology modules; Matlis duality. 1  2  M. HATAMKHANI  Note that faa (M ) = fa (M ). For a prime ideal p ∈ Spec(R) \ V(a), the a-adjusted depth of M at p, denoted adja depth Mp , is defined by adja depth Mp := depth Mp + ht((a + p)/p). The b-minimum a-adjusted depth of M , denoted by λba (M ), is defined by λba (M ) := inf{adja depth Mp : p ∈ Spec(R) \ V(b)} = inf{depth Mp + ht((a + p)/p) : p ∈ Spec(R) \ V(b)}. Another important theorem is Faltings’ Annihilator Theorem, asserts that under milds restrictions on R, the invariants λba (M ) and fab (M ) are equal, see [F2]. In section 3, we intoduce the concept of the Artinianness dimension g a (A) of an Artinian R- module A relative to a by g a (A) := inf{i ∈ N : Hia (A) is not Artinian}, which is in some sence dual to the finiteness dimension and prove that a dual of two Faltings’ Theorems for local homology modules of an Artinian module over a semi-local complete ring. As a consequence of this, we show that, if A is an Artinian module over semi-local complete ring R and j is an integer such that Hia (A) is Artinian for all i < j, then the set CoassR (Hja (A)) is finite. For a non-negative integer n, Bahmanpour et al., in [BNS] introduced the notion of the nth finiteness dimension fan (M ) of M relative to a by fan (M ) := inf{faRp (Mp ) : p ∈ Supp(M/aM ) and dim R/p ≥ n}. In the last section, we define the nth Artinianness dimension gna (A) of A relative to a by gna (A) = inf{g aRp (p A) : p ∈ Spec(R) and dim R/p ≥ n} and show that the least integer i such that Hia (A) is not minimax, equals to g1a (A). This implies that CoassR (Hgaa (A) (A)) is a finite set, whenever R is semi-local complete ring. 1  For any ideal b of R, the radical of b, denoted by Rad(b), is defined to be the set {x ∈ R : xn ∈ b for some n ∈ N}. Recall that an R- module M is called a- cofinite if Supp(M ) ⊆ V(a) and ExtiR (R/a, M ) is finitely generated for all i ≥ 0. The concept of a- cofinite modules were introduced by Hartshorne [Ha] and has been proved to be an important tool in the study of modules over a commutativt ring. Moreover, an R- module M is said to be minimax, if there exists a finitely generated submodule N of M , such that M/N is Artinian. The class of minimax modules was introduced by Zöschinger [Zo]. Bahmanpour et al., in [BNS] proved that fa1 (M ) := inf{i ∈ N : Hai (M ) is not minimax}.  In this section we establish the dual of the above equality for local homology modules.
