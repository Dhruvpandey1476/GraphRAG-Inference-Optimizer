LINEAR SYZYGIES OF CURVES WITH PRESCRIBED GONALITY  arXiv:1610.04424v1 [math.AG] 14 Oct 2016  GAVRIL FARKAS AND MICHAEL KEMENY Abstract. We prove two statements concerning the linear strand of the minimal free resolution of a k-gonal curve C of genus g. Firstly, we show that a general curve C of genus g of nonmaximal gonality k ≤ g+1 satisfies Schreyer’s Conjecture, that is, bg−k,1 (C, KC ) = g −k. This is 2 a statement going beyond Green’s Conjecture and predicts that all highest order linear syzygies in the canonical embedding of C are determined by the syzygies of the (k − 1)-dimensional scroll containing C. Secondly, we formulate an optimal effective version of the Gonality Conjecture and prove it for general k-gonal curves. This generalizes the asymptotic Gonality Conjecture proved by Ein-Lazarsfeld and improves results of Rathmann in the case where C is a general curve of fixed gonality.  0. Introduction 1.
