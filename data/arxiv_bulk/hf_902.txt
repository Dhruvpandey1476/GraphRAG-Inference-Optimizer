​objects.​ ​ ​Sequential​ ​stimuli​ ​are​ ​multiple​ ​detectable​ ​phenomena​ ​in​ ​an environment​ ​that​ ​occur​ ​during​ ​different​ ​time​ ​ranges,​ ​usually​ ​proceeding​ ​one​ ​after​ ​another.​
