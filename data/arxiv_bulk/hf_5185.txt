arXiv:1707.01328v2 [math.GR] 17 Jul 2017  QUASI-SEMISIMPLE ELEMENTS F. DIGNE AND J. MICHEL  Abstract. We study quasi-semisimple elements of disconnected reductive algebraic groups over an algebraically closed field. We describe their centralizers, define isolated and quasi-isolated quasi-semisimple elements and classify their conjugacy classes.  Introduction In this paper we study conjugacy classes and centralizers of quasi-semisimple elements in disconnected reductive groups over algebraically closed fields. Let G be an algebraic group such that the connected component G0 is reductive. Let G1 be a connected component of G and H be the subgroup of G generated by G1 . Then G0 -conjugacy classes of G1 coincide with H-conjugacy classes, and Gconjugacy classes of G1 are unions of orbits of the finite group G/H on H-conjugacy classes. Thus it is reasonable to first consider the case where G is generated by G1 . This is closely related to the study of algebraic automorphisms of G0 : if G0 is adjoint, the elements of G1 can be interpreted as a class of automorphisms of G0 modulo inner automorphisms. According to these considerations, in what follows we will use the following setting: we denote by σ an element of a (disconnected) reductive group with identity component denoted by G, and we study the conjugacy classes in the coset G1 := G · σ. We consider algebraic groups over some algebraically closed field k of characteristic p , where p is 0 or a prime number. We denote by nσ the smallest power of σ which acts by an inner automorphism on G. A quasi-semisimple element is an element of G1 which stabilizes a pair T ⊂ B of a maximal torus of G0 and a Borel subgroup containing it; let T1 be the normalizer of this pair in G1 . Since all such pairs are conjugate, and we are interested in quasisemisimple elements up to conjugacy, we study the elements of T1 . An element σ ∈ T1 acts on W := NG (T)/T; its order as an automorphism of T is nσ . If G is semisimple, nσ is also the order of the action of σ on W since an algebraic automorphism of G stabilizing T and trivial on W is inner. Our results are as follows. In the first section, we study the centralizer Gσ of σ, generalizing the work of Steinberg [St68] when G is semisimple simply connected, and of [DM94] which deals with the connected component (Gσ )0 .
