Two Dimensional Angle of Arrival Estimation  arXiv:1604.00594v1 [cs.CE] 3 Apr 2016  Santhosh Kumar, Pradip Sircar Department of Electrical Engineering Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur Kanpur 208016, Uttar Pradesh, India  1  Introduction  We present a new method for the estimation of two dimensional (2D) angles of arrival (AOAs), namely, azimuth and incidence angles of multiple narrowband signals of same frequency in the far field of antenna array. In this report, we propose an algorithm which uses an L-shape array configuration [1]. We extend the polynomial based approach presented in [2] to two dimensional AOA estimation.
