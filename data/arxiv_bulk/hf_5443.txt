BOUNDS FOR TRACES OF HECKE OPERATORS AND APPLICATIONS TO MODULAR AND ELLIPTIC CURVES OVER A FINITE FIELD  arXiv:1710.09869v1 [math.NT] 26 Oct 2017  IAN PETROW  Abstract. We give an upper bound for the trace of a Hecke operator acting on the space of holomorphic cusp forms with respect to a congruence subgroup. Such an estimate has applications to the analytic theory of elliptic curves over a finite field, going beyond the Riemann hypothesis over finite fields. As the main tool to prove our bound on traces of Hecke operators, we develop a Petersson formula for newforms for general nebentype characters.  1. Introduction 1.1. Statement of Results.
