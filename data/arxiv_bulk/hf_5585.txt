arXiv:1305.0093v1 [math.AC] 1 May 2013  Weighted multidegrees of polynomial automorphisms over a domain Shigeru Kuroda  ∗  Abstract The notion of the weighted degree of a polynomial is a basic tool in Affine Algebraic Geometry. In this paper, we study the properties of the weighted multidegrees of polynomial automorphisms by a new approach which focuses on stable coordinates. We also present some applications of the generalized Shestakov-Umirbaev theory.  1  Introduction  Throughout this paper, k denotes an arbitrary domain unless otherwise stated.
