Introduction Let G be a finite group with no nontrivial representations of degree less than D. Such groups are called D-quasirandom. In [4], Gowers showed that if G is D-quasirandom, then ! r |A||B||C| |G||A||B||C| +O #{(x, y, xy) ∈ A×B×C : x, y ∈ G} = |G| D for every A, B, C ⊂ G. The quantity |A||B||C|/|G| is the number of triples (x, y, xy) that we would expect to lie in the Cartesian product of three random subsets of G of the same size as A, B, and C. Thus, whenever |A||B||C| is sufficiently large, the set A × B × C contains close to the expected number of triples (x, y, xy). Gowers asked in [4] whether a similar result holds in quasirandom groups for configurations other than (x, y, xy), in particular for threeterm progressions (x, xy, xy 2). This was answered in the affirmative by Tao in [9] for the family of groups SLd (Fq ) with d bounded, all of whose nontrivial representations have degree ≫ q d−1 (see [5]). Liebeck and Shalev’s second theorem concerns collections of groups with unbounded rank: Theorem 4.2 (Liebeck and Shalev, Theorem 1.2 of [7]). Fix s > 0. There exists an integer r(s) such that for any finite quasisimple group of Lie type G of rank at least r(s), we have that ζG (s) → 1 as |G| → ∞. The group SLd (Fq ) has rank d − 1, so that for every ε > 0, Theorem 4.2 furnishes a d(ε) such that whenever d ≥ d(ε), we have that ζSLd (Fq ) (ε) − 1 ≤ 1. Then ζSLd (Fq ) (1) − 1 ≪ q −(1−ε)(d−1) , since SLd (Fq ) is ≫ q d−1 -quasirandom. This bound and Theorem 1.1 imply (5), completing the proof of Corollary 1.3. To prove Corollary 1.2, we use the classification of finite simple groups. Let r(2/3) be as in Theorem 4.2. Then for all finite simple groups of Lie type of rank at least r(2/3), we have that ζG (2/3)−1 ≪ 1. To deal with the finitely many families of finite simple groups of Lie type of rank less than r(2/3), we apply Theorem 4.1. The only family of finite simple groups of Lie type whose corresponding Coxeter number is less than 3 is PSL2 (Fq ) (see [2].) Thus, Theorem 4.1 implies that if G is a finite simple group of Lie type of rank less than r(2/3), then ζG (2/3) − 1 ≪ 1 as well, since such groups G can have one of only finitely many Lie types. Since ζAn (2/3) − 1 ≪ n−2/3 ≪ 1, we thus have that ζG (2/3) − 1 ≪ 1 for all nonabelian finite simple groups G other than PSL2 (Fq ). It follows that if G 6= PSL2 (Fq ) is a D-quasirandom nonabelian finite simple group, then ζG (1) − 1 ≪ D −1/3 . This combined with Theorem 1.1 proves Corollary 1.2 when G 6= PSL2 (Fq ), and the case G = PSL2 (Fq ) follows from Tao’s result (1).
