We define a new bandwidth-dependent kernel density estimator that improves existing convergence rates for the bias, and preserves that of the variation, when the error is measured in L1 . No additional assumptions are imposed to the extant literature. Keywords and phrases. Kernel density estimation, higher order kernels, bias reduction.
