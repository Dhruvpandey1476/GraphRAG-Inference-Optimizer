REPRESENTATIONS OF GROUPS WITH CAT(0) FIXED POINT PROPERTY  arXiv:1803.10175v1 [math.GR] 27 Mar 2018  OLGA VARGHESE Abstract. We show that certain representations over fields with positive characteristic of groups having CAT(0) fixed point property FBÃn have finite image. In particular, we obtain rigidity results for representations of the following groups: the special linear group over Z, SLk (Z), the special automorphism group of a free group, SAut(Fk ), the mapping class group of a closed orientable surface, Mod(Σg ), and many other groups. In the case of characteristic zero we show that low dimensional complex representations of groups having CAT(0) fixed point property FBÃn have finite image if they always have compact closure.  1. Preliminaries 2.1. Discrete valuations. For background material on discrete valuations and valuation rings we refer the reader to [Neu99]. Let K be a field with a discrete valuation ν, i.e. ν is a a surjective map ν ∶ K ∗ → Z satisfying ν(ab) = ν(a) + ν(b) and ν(a + b) ≥ min {ν(a), ν(b)} for all a, b ∈ K ∗ , with the convention that ν(0) = ∞. An element π ∈ K ∗ is called a uniformizer if ν(π) = 1. By Oν = {a ∈ K ∣ v(a) ≥ 0} we denote the discrete valuation ring of K with respect to ν. In particular, we have a ∈ Oν or a−1 ∈ Oν for every a ∈ K ∗ . We need the notion of algebraic elements in rings, which is a as follows. Definition 2.1. Let S be a ring and R ⊆ S a subring of S. So ρ(G) is a finitely generated torsion group and by Theorem 5.1 this group is finite.  6.4. Proof of Theorem D. Proof. Let ρ ∶ G → GLn+1 (C) be a linear representation of G. Let {g1 , . . . , gl } be a finite generating set of G and let Cρ be the subfield of C generated by the coefficients of the matrices ρ(gi ) for i = 1, . . . , l. Hence the field Cρ is finitely generated over its prime field Q. We get ρ(G) ⊆ GLn+1 (Cρ ). Our goal is to show, that the eigenvalues of ρ(g) for g ∈ G are roots of unity. By Proposition E the eigenvalues of ρ(g) for g ∈ G are integral over Z in Cρ . We have Q ⊆ Q ⊆ Cρ . By Proposition 2.7 the integral closure Q in Cρ is a number field and the eigenvalues of ρ(g) are in ∗ ∗ Z ⊆ Q . Since ρ has image with compact closure, the eigenvalues of ρ(g) for g ∈ G have absolute value 1 and since each representation of degree n + 1 has image with compact closure it follows by Proposition 2.8 that the eigenvalues of ρ(g) for g ∈ G are roots of unity. Further ρ(g) is diagonalizable and since each eigenvalue has finite order the matrix ρ(g) has finite order.
