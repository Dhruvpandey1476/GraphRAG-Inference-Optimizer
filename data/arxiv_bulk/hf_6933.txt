THE DEGREE OF THE ALGEBRA OF COVARIANTS OF A BINARY FORM  arXiv:1304.5328v1 [math.RA] 19 Apr 2013  LEONID BEDRATYUK AND NADIA ILASH  Abstract. We calculate the degree of the algebra of covariants Cd for binary d-form. Also, for the degree we obtain its integral representation and asymptotic behavior.  1. Introduction Let R = R0 + R1 + · · · be a finitely generated graded complex algebra, R0 = C. Denote by P(R, z) =  ∞ X  dim Rj z j ,  j=0  its Poincare series. The number deg(R) := lim (1 − z)r P(R, z), z→1  is called the degree of the algebra R. Here r is the transcendence degree of the quotient field of R over C. The first two terms of the Laurent series expansion of P(R, z) at the point z = 1 have the following form  P(R, z) =  deg(R) ψ(R) + + ··· (1 − z)r (1 − z)r−1  The numbers deg(R), ψ(R) are important characteristics of the algebra R. For instance, if R is an algebra ψ(R) of invariants of a finite group G then deg(R)−1 is order of the group G and 2 is the number of deg(R) the pseudo-reflections in G, see [1]. Let Vd be standard d + 1-dimensional complex representation of SL2 and let Id := C[Vd ]SL2 be the  corresponding algebra of invariants. In the language of the classical invariant theory the algebra Id is called the algebra of invariants for d-binary form of degree d. Also, he had found an integral representation and asymptotic behavior for Hilbert’s constants. For this purpose Springer used an explicit formula for computation of the Poincare series P(Id , z) derived in his paper [4]. Let Cd be the algebra of the covariants if the binary d-form, i.e. Cd ∼ = C[V1 ⊕ Vd ]SL2 . In the present  paper we calculate deg(Cd ) and ψ(Cd ). Also, we calculate an integral representation and asymptotic  behavior of the constants.
