A subset B of a group G is called a difference basis of G if each element g ∈ G can be written as the difference g = ab−1 of some elements a, b ∈ B. The smallest cardinality |B| ofpa difference basis B ⊂ G is called the difference size of G and is denoted by ∆[G]. The fraction ð[G] := ∆[G]/ |G| is called the difference characteristic √ of G. We prove that for every n ∈ N the dihedral group D2n of order 2n has the difference characteristic 2 ≤ ð[D2n ] ≤ √48 ≈ 1.983. Moreover, if n ≥ 2 · 1015 , then ð[D2n ] < √4 ≈ 1.633. Also we 586 6 calculate the difference sizes and characteristics of all dihedral groups of cardinality ≤ 80.  A subset B of a group G is called a difference basis for a subset A ⊂ G if each element a ∈ A can be written as a = xy −1 for some x, y ∈ B. The smallest cardinality of a difference basis for A is called the difference size of A and is denoted by ∆[A]. For example, the set {0, 1, 4, 6} is a difference basis for the interval A = [−6, 6] ∩ Z witnessing that ∆[A] ≤ 4.
